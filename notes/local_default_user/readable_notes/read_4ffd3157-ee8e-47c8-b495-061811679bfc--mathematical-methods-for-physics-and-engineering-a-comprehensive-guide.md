In \(16.7\), if, at some point z = z0, the functions p\(z\) and q\(z\) are finite and can be expressed as complex power series \(see section 4.5\), i.e. p\(z\) = ∞ n=0 pn\(z − z0\) n , q\(z\) = ∞ n=0 qn\(z − z0\) n , then p\(z\) and q\(z\) are said to be analytic at z = z0, and this point is called an ordinary point of the ODE. If, however, p\(z\) or q\(z\), or both, diverge at z = z0 then it is called a singular point of the ODE.
