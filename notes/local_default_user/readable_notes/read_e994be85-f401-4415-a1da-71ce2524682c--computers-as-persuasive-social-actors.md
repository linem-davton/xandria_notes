Like other digital pets, Pikachu required care and feeding, but with a twist: the device contained a pedometer that could register and record the owner’s movements. For the digital creature to thrive, its owner had to be physically active on a consistent basis. The owner had to walk, run, or jump—anything to activate the pedometer. Pocket Pikachu is a simple example of a computing device functioning as a persuasive social actor.
